Your Arts

  • Stab — foes adjacent before and after your move die.
  • Lunge — move straight toward a foe to skewer it (needs spear in hand).
  • Leap — jump 2 tiles for 50 vigor, then a 2-turn cooldown. Kills restore 15 vigor — but not kills made mid-leap — and vigor refills at each new depth.
  • Throw — hurl your spear up to its range; walk onto it to reclaim it. No spear, no lunge.
  • Bash — knock a neighbor back one tile and stun it. Fire rifts and the void kill instantly.
  • Hold — pass your turn.
  • Preview — press and hold any tile (or an enemy) to preview the outcome — who dies, where you'd land, what still threatens you. Release without acting; a quick tap commits.

The Dead of Helheim

  • Draugr — strikes any adjacent tile.
  • Bone Archer — shoots straight lines, 2–5 tiles. Get close or break its line.
  • Surtling — lobs embers that burst one turn later, scorching adjacent tiles. Bash embers away!
  • Völva — sears a line up to 5 tiles whenever her staff glows; rests one turn after each blast.
  • Ancients (gold ring) take two blows; Deathless (crimson ring) take three.

Red dashes mark threatened tiles. Glory: +1 per kill, +10 for descending without touching the runestone, and +1 per living foe if you descend with none dead — pacifist descents are a real path. The dungeon has no end — each depth spawns more and deadlier foes. How deep can you go?
